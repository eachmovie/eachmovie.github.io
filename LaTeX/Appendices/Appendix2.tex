\section*{Factual errata}

In the published proceedings of ACM RecSys 2026, my paper ``EachMovie: archaeology of a first latent recommender system''~\cite{DeTreville2026EachMovie_paper} includes two small mistakes, corrected here.
\begin{enumerate}

\item The published paper references a ``permanent'' EachMovie artifact repository at the Center for Open Science. Shortly \emph{after} the paper's camera-ready deadline, though, the Center announced they can no longer host such repositories. That repository now contains permanent forwarding pointers to two fresh repositories on GitHub~\cite{EachMovieGitHub} and Zenodo~\cite{EachMovieZenodo}.

  \item The published paper says the EachMovie patent~\cite{DeTrevillePatent2000} was issued June~30, 2000, but that should be June~\emph{20}, 2000. (United States patents are issued on Tuesdays, not Fridays.)
\end{enumerate}
