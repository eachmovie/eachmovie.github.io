Want to see a movie? Overwhelmed with the choices? Let us help! Just tell us what you thought of movies and videos you've already seen, and we'll steer you to new releases that you should like.

You can use this FREE service to choose your next video, plan dates, or organize kids' night out!

  • You start by telling us who you are (just some basic info).
  • Next, you rate the movies that you've already seen. Be honest; no one's looking!
  • Then, we predict what you are likely to think of all the movies and videos you still haven't seen. Say goodbye to decision trauma!

Just so you know ...
This service is an independent research project, not an undercover attempt by one of the big movie chains to get you to see their movies. We're trying to apply some technologies from our labs to solving real-world problems like the "video-store daze" (where you wander for hours trying to find a video that you haven't seen yet and might enjoy). If you have constructive suggestions about how we can improve the service, please join our virtual development team ... and you might even win valuable prizes!
