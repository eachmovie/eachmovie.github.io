Welcome to EachMovie, where we know what you like! We tell you which movies to see and which to stay away from, chosen from a database of current first-run movies and video releases in North America.

Register first, then we'll predict your future favorites! The more you correct us, the smarter we get! It's fun, it's free, and no salesman will call. We don't give out our mailing list, and we'll take you off ours the moment you ask!

You can find EachMovie at http://www.pigtail.com/each/movie/.
