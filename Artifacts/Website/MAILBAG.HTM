Our Mailbag

From: MCVL

I agree with Roger Ebert about EachMovie (www.eachmovie.com) -- I've been using it for two months, and it's eerily good at predicting what I'll like. Movie Critic was new to me, so I thought I'd compare. I now have over 200 movies rated in each and EachMovie wins, hands down.
EachMovie is never more than one notch off in either direction, while Movie Critic often tells me I'm going to love something I hate, or vice versa. I attribute the difference to EachMovie's decision to have me rate only movies I've seen recently, so my response is based on the film itself rather than a hazy recollection.
I also hate it that Movie Critic is so slow to incorporate my changes. One of my favorite games with EachMovie is to enter a bogus rating, just to see how it affects the predictions; when I revert to my true opinion, EachMovie recalculates all its predictions instantly. Movie Critic, on the other hand, "carefully absorbs" my ratings over the next day or so.
In sum, I'll stick with EachMovie -- it's a better predictor, and more fun to use.
